Workspace Overview

This Coda workspace is directly wired to the IntegrateWise Spine through our custom Pack. Updates to any database will populate here, and actions taken here propagate back to the execution layer.

Quick Directory

  • 🏠 Home: Current page. Overview and navigation directory.
  • 📚 Spine: The Book of Records and Book of Projects relational database views.
  • 🧠 Personal Memory: Context, preferences, and behavioral signals for active developers.
  • 🏛️ Org Memory: Canonical institutional memory, staging, and approval queues.
  • ⚙️ Ops: Live pipeline metrics, connector statuses, and task flows.
  • 📈 Progress: Service Integration (SI) plan progress, ARR status, and spotlight metrics.
