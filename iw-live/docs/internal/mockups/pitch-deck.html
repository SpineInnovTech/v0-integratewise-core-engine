Governance

L4
Knowledge & Assets
Entity graph, semantic store, retrieval — the queryable shape of organizational memory.
L5
Playbooks
BYOT templates — procedural memory, workflow patterns, decision rights, escalation paths.
L6
Operations
Event sourcing, CQRS, queues, schedulers — the operational nervous system.
L7
Execution
Gateway · 100+ connectors · Browser Agent (Playwright, cua-driver) · external systems — where the world actually changes.
↳ Design principles
Each layer is a lens, not a silo.
Event-sourced
Every fact is captured once and replayable forever.
CQRS-separated
Reads scale independently from writes. No lock contention.
BYOM · BYOT
Bring your own model. Bring your own template. Data sovereignty preserved.
Governable by default
Policy, lineage, audit at L3 — not bolted on later.
L1 Workbench → L7 Execution · Continuity at every layer
