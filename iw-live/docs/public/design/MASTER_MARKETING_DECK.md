## WHAT INTEGRATEWISE IS (30-second version)

IntegrateWise connects every tool you use into one workspace. It normalizes your data into a single source of truth (the Spine), assembles a complete 360-degree view of every customer/entity, and uses AI to surface evidence-backed insights — but the AI only acts with your approval, and only remembers what you verify.
