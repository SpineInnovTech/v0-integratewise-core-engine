# IntegrateWise — Campaign Playbook

> Campaign structures for launch and ongoing growth.
> All messaging in feature/user language. No architecture jargon.

---

## Campaign 1: Launch Campaign — "Stop Being the Human API"

### Objective

Drive 500 email signups and 50 demo bookings in the first 30 days.

### Audience Segments

| Segment                 | Pain                                         | Message                                                                | Channel                                           |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| CS Leaders (SaaS)       | Team spends 3+ hours/day gathering data      | "Your CSMs deserve better than 8 tabs"                                 | LinkedIn, Email                                   |
| Founders (10-50 person) | Checks 12 tabs every morning                 | "Stop checking 12 tabs. Run your business from one screen."            | Twitter/X, LinkedIn                               |
| India SMB Owners        | Juggles WhatsApp + Tally + Razorpay + Sheets | "All your tools in one place — connected and intelligent. ₹999/month." | WhatsApp groups, YouTube Hindi, Instagram         |
| UAE FZE Founders        | 10 apps before first meeting                 | "Run your FZE from one dashboard. $49/month."                          | LinkedIn, coworking spaces, free zone communities |
| CAs / Accountants       | Tracks 50 clients across spreadsheets        | "Never miss a filing. See every client's status in one view."          | CA networks, WhatsApp groups                      |

### Campaign Assets

| Asset                | Format         | Purpose                                                       |
| -------------------- | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hero video (90 sec)  | MP4 / YouTube  | Show the before/after: 12 tabs → one screen                   |
| Product demo (3 min) | MP4 / Loom     | Walk through connecting tools → complete view → AI insight    |
| Pain infographic     | PNG / carousel | "The Human API Problem" — time wasted per role                |
| Feature cards (5)    | PNG / carousel | One card per key feature, user language                       |
| Founder story video  | MP4 / YouTube  | "I saved an $8M account by building what should have existed" |
| Landing page         | Web            | Hero + problem + features + 3 doors + CTA                     |

### Channel Plan (Week 1-4)

**Week 1: Awareness**

- LinkedIn: 3 pain-point posts (Human API problem, time wasted, AI trust gap)
- Twitter/X: 5 posts (founder story thread, product teaser, pain stats)
- Email: Announce to existing contacts — "We're live. Here's what we built."
- YouTube: Hero video launch

**Week 2: Education**

- LinkedIn: 3 feature posts (complete view, AI insights, verified memory)
- Twitter/X: 5 posts (feature highlights, demo clips, customer reactions)
- Email: Feature deep-dive — "One click. Everything about any account."
- Blog: "The Human API Problem — and how to fix it"

**Week 3: Social Proof**

- LinkedIn: 3 posts (pilot results, customer quotes, before/after metrics)
- Twitter/X: 5 posts (metrics, testimonials, demo booking CTA)
- Email: Customer story — "How [Company] cut meeting prep from 4 hours to 12 minutes"
- Blog: "Why 76% of enterprises require human oversight on AI"

**Week 4: Conversion**

- LinkedIn: 3 posts (pricing, demo CTA, urgency — "Early access closing soon")
- Twitter/X: 5 posts (pricing, last call, founder direct message)
- Email: Final push — "Last week for early access pricing"
- Retargeting: Website visitors who viewed pricing but didn't book

### Success Metrics

| Metric               | Target |
| -------------------- | ------ |
| Email signups        | 500    |
| Demo bookings        | 50     |
| Pilots started       | 10     |
| Paid conversions     | 5      |
| LinkedIn impressions | 50,000 |
| Website visitors     | 5,000  |

## Campaign 4: Enterprise — "The CS Platform That Actually Knows Your Customers"

### Objective

10 enterprise pilots at $299-999/mo within 12 weeks.

### Audience

VP Customer Success, CS Directors, CROs at B2B SaaS companies (50-500 employees).

### Key Messages

- "Your CSMs check 8 tools to understand one account. What if they checked one?"
- "Catch churn 6 weeks before the renewal. With evidence."
- "AI that shows its work. Every insight backed by data from your actual tools."
- "The only system where AI memory is verified by humans before it becomes a fact."

### Channel Plan

| Channel                 | What                                                | Frequency    |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| LinkedIn (targeted)     | Thought leadership, CS pain posts, product features | 3x/week      |
| Cold email (CS leaders) | Personalized outreach to VP CS at target companies  | 20/week      |
| SaaStr community        | Posts, comments, event participation                | Weekly       |
| Webinar                 | "How to detect churn before health scores move"     | Monthly      |
| Case study              | Pilot results with before/after metrics             | As available |
| Founder-led demos       | Personal demo with prospect's own data              | On demand    |

### Enterprise Features to Highlight

| Feature                   | Message                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Complete account view     | "Everything about any account — CRM, support, usage, billing, AI insights — in one click."                      |
| AI insights with evidence | "The system noticed usage dropped 42% and there's a renewal in 45 days. Here's the evidence."                   |
| Verified AI memory        | "When AI learns something about your accounts, it asks your team first. Only confirmed facts enter the system." |
| Portfolio health map      | "Every account on one grid. Colored by health. Sized by value. The red ones need attention."                    |
| Duplicate resolution      | "Same company, three records across three tools. We detect it. You decide: merge or keep separate."             |
| Board-ready metrics       | "Retention forecast, revenue at risk, health distribution — one-click export for the board."                    |
