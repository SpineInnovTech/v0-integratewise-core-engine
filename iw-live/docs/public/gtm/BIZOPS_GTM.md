## Positioning

### One-liner

"IntegrateWise BizOps: Your entire business in one dashboard — connected, resolved, and reasoned."

### Positioning Statement

For founders and operators of 10–200 person companies who are drowning in SaaS tabs, IntegrateWise BizOps is a Knowledge Workspace that connects your tools, resolves your data into unified entities, and surfaces the insights that matter — so you can run your business from one screen instead of twelve.

### Key Differentiators vs. Alternatives

| Alternative           | Limitation                            | IntegrateWise Advantage                             |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Databox / Geckoboard  | Display-only dashboards, no reasoning | Twin triggers surface _why_ metrics changed         |
| Notion / spreadsheets | Manual data entry, stale by noon      | Auto-sync from 70+ sources, always current          |
| Internal dashboards   | Engineering time, maintenance burden  | Zero-code setup, managed connectors                 |
| Causal / Baremetrics  | Billing metrics only                  | Cross-functional: revenue + product + support + ops |

## Launch Strategy

### Phase 1: Founder Beta (Weeks 1–6)

- 20 founders from personal network + indie hacker communities
- Free access, weekly feedback calls
- Goal: validate morning briefing and Founder Cockpit UX
- Channels: Twitter/X, Indie Hackers, founder Slack groups

### Phase 2: Product Hunt Launch (Week 8)

- Position as "The governed workspace for founders who hate tab-switching"
- Demo video: 90-second walkthrough of morning briefing flow
- Launch day support: founder available for comments, live demo link
- Goal: 500 signups, 50 activated (connected 3+ tools)

### Phase 3: Content-Led Growth (Weeks 8–16)

- Weekly blog: "How I run my $2M company from one dashboard"
- SEO targets: "founder dashboard," "business workspace," "SaaS metrics dashboard"
- Guest posts on First Round Review, SaaStr, Lenny's Newsletter
- Goal: 200 organic signups/month

### Phase 4: Paid Acquisition (Week 12+)

- Google Ads: "founder dashboard," "business metrics tool," "SaaS business workspace"
- LinkedIn Ads: targeting founders and COOs at 10–200 person companies
- Retargeting: website visitors who viewed pricing but didn't sign up
- Target CAC: < $150 for Starter tier
