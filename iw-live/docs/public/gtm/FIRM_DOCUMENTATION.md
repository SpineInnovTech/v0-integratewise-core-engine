## What IntegrateWise Is

IntegrateWise is a Knowledge Workspace that connects every tool a business uses into one intelligent system. It watches your data, surfaces what matters, and only acts with your approval. Your business gets smarter every day — without retraining anything.

**Category:** Knowledge Workspace
**Not:** A platform, an OS, a tool, or a SaaS dashboard.

---

## The Problem We Solve

Every business runs on 5–200 tools. Data is fragmented. Nobody has a single view of their accounts, clients, or customers. People spend 2–4 hours a day gathering data across tabs before they can do actual work.

AI made it worse. ChatGPT, Copilot, and Gemini all auto-write their memory. They guess your context. They invent data. 82% of consumers see AI data collection as a threat. 76% of enterprises require human oversight. Nobody trusts AI without verification.

IntegrateWise fixes both problems:

1. Connect your tools → see everything in one screen
2. AI thinks in full context — your data, your conversations, your accumulated knowledge — and only acts with your approval

## What the Product Does (Features)

### Connect All Your Tools in Minutes

- 70+ integrations across CRM, billing, support, analytics, marketing, communication, productivity, project management, engineering, commerce, and AI
- No coding, no IT team needed — connect via OAuth in 2 minutes per tool
- Data is automatically cleaned, deduplicated, and organized
- New data syncs automatically — every 15 minutes to every 4 hours depending on plan

### One Complete View of Any Account, Client, or Customer

- Click on any account and see everything: facts from your CRM, conversations from email and Slack, alerts from the system, verified AI knowledge, goals, and relationships
- No more opening 4 tools to prepare for a meeting
- Works for SaaS accounts, CA clients, hotel guests, freelance projects, retail customers — any entity you manage

### AI That Watches Your Business and Tells You What Needs Attention

- The system watches patterns across all your connected tools and surfaces what matters
- "This account's health dropped — here's why and what you could do"
- "This renewal is coming up and there are warning signs"
- "This customer went quiet — they used to be active weekly"
- Every alert comes with evidence — which data, from which tool, how confident
- Maximum 3 insights per entity per day — no alert fatigue

### AI That Thinks in Full Context

The AI doesn't just read raw data from your CRM. It reads everything:

- **Your data** (Truth) — accounts, contacts, deals, invoices, tasks from every connected tool
- **Your conversations** (Context) — emails, Slack messages, meeting notes, documents, PDFs
- **Your accumulated knowledge** (Knowledge Memory) — AI session summaries, decisions, preferences, insights — all governed by your approval

All three layers meet in one complete view per entity. The AI reasons across all of them. That's why it catches things no single tool can see — it connects a usage drop (data) with a support escalation (data) with a budget freeze mentioned in an email (context) with a decision you approved last month (knowledge memory).

### AI That Only Writes to Memory With Your Approval

- When AI discovers something about your business, it proposes it to you first
- You see what it found, where it came from, and how confident it is
- You approve, reject, or edit — only what you confirm becomes part of your business knowledge
- No hallucinated facts. No AI inventing a customer commitment that doesn't exist
- Full audit trail on every piece of knowledge

### Your Business Gets Smarter Every Day

- Every day, as you use IntegrateWise, your verified knowledge grows
- New customer interactions, new decisions, new insights — all accumulating
- After a month, the system knows your business better than any new hire

## The TruthLayer Advantage

TruthLayer governs what enters Knowledge Memory — the AI's accumulated learning about your business. But the AI's intelligence comes from all three layers working together:

- **Truth** — structured data from your connected tools (CRM, billing, support, analytics). Flows in automatically.
- **Context** — unstructured data from emails, documents, Slack, meetings. Flows in automatically.
- **Knowledge Memory** — AI session summaries, decisions, insights. This is what TruthLayer governs.

The AI reads from all three. It thinks in context. It reasons across your data, your conversations, and your accumulated knowledge. TruthLayer ensures that when AI learns something new, it doesn't just write it down — it proposes it to you first. You approve, reject, or edit. Only confirmed knowledge enters the memory.

This matters because:

- Truth without context is just raw data — you can't understand why an account is at risk without reading the emails
- Context without knowledge memory means the AI forgets what it learned yesterday
- Knowledge memory without approval means hallucinated facts pollute your system

All three layers compound over time. That's why it's called a Knowledge Workspace — the workspace itself gets smarter every day.

**Why it matters:** ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, and Mem0 all let AI write to memory automatically. None have triage scoring, approval gates, or open-read architecture. IntegrateWise is the only system where AI thinks in full context and only writes to memory with your approval.
