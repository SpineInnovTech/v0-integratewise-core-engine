# IntegrateWise — Sales Playbook

> Feature-first selling. No architecture jargon in the conversation.
> Speak in what the buyer gets, not how it works.

---

## The Sales Conversation Framework

### Opening (30 seconds)

"How many tools does your team use to understand one account/client/customer?"

Wait for the answer. It's always 5+. Then:

"What if you could see everything about any account in one click — facts, conversations, alerts, knowledge, goals, relationships — all assembled automatically?"

### The Three Hooks (pick based on buyer)

**Hook 1 — For the person drowning in tabs:**
"Connect your tools once. See everything in one screen. Your morning briefing is ready before you are."

**Hook 2 — For the person who missed a signal:**
"The system watches your connected tools continuously. When something needs attention — a customer going quiet, a renewal approaching, usage dropping — it tells you. With evidence. Before it becomes a crisis."

**Hook 3 — For the person who doesn't trust AI:**
"Every insight comes with evidence — which data, from which tool, how recent. The AI doesn't just read your CRM — it reads your emails, your documents, your meeting notes, and your accumulated knowledge. It thinks in full context. And when it learns something new, it asks you first. You approve what enters the knowledge. You reject what doesn't. Nothing writes to memory without your say."

## Demo Script (15 Minutes)

**Minute 0-2: The Hook**
"Let me show you what your accounts look like when everything is connected."

**Minute 2-5: Connect + Complete View**
Show a real account with data from multiple tools assembled in one view. Point out: "This data came from Salesforce. This came from Zendesk. This came from Stripe. All assembled automatically."

**Minute 5-8: AI Insights**
Show an AI insight firing on an account. "See this? The system noticed usage dropped 42% and there's a renewal in 45 days. It's telling you to act now — with evidence."

**Minute 8-10: Three Layers + TruthLayer**
Show the complete view with all three layers. "This is the data from your CRM — that's Truth. This is the email thread from last week — that's Context. And this is what the AI learned from your last session and you approved — that's Knowledge Memory. The AI reads all three to give you this insight. And when it learns something new, it asks you first."

**Minute 10-12: Portfolio View (for managers)**
Show the health map. "Every account, colored by health, sized by value. The red ones need attention. The AI is already telling you why."

**Minute 12-14: Q&A**

**Minute 14-15: Next Steps**
"Want to see this with your own data? We can connect 3 of your tools in 30 minutes and show you your accounts unified."

## Pilot Success Criteria

A pilot is successful if:

1. They connect at least 1 tool during the demo or within 48 hours
2. They see their own data in the complete account view (not demo data)
3. They say "this would save me [X] hours per week" or equivalent
4. They agree to a 30-day trial or paid subscription
5. They provide a testimonial quote or agree to be a reference

If 2 out of 3 pilots meet criteria 1-3, the product-market fit signal is strong.
