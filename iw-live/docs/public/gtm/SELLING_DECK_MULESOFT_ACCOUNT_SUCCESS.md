## Slide 1: The Universal Problem

Every person who manages accounts, clients, or customers faces the same problem:

- **Data fragmentation** — account data lives across 5-20 different tools
- **No unified health signal** — is the account healthy? Nobody knows until it's too late
- **Manual prep** — hours assembling data from multiple systems for a single meeting
- **Reactive detection** — by the time you spot a risk, the relationship is already damaged

This is the same pain whether you're a CSM at a SaaS company, a CA managing 50 clients, a hotel manager tracking guest satisfaction, or a freelancer juggling 10 projects.
