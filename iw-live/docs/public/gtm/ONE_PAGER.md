# IntegrateWise

**A Knowledge Workspace that connects your tools, builds a single source of truth, and gives you an AI that thinks in full context — your data, your conversations, and your knowledge — before it says or does anything.**

---

## Why IntegrateWise Exists

Your business runs on dozens of tools. Your CRM knows the deal. Your support desk knows the tickets. Your billing system knows the payments. Your email knows the relationship. Your AI knows none of it.

You are the one connecting the dots — switching tabs, copy-pasting context, briefing every tool and every AI from scratch. Every morning. Every account. Every decision.

IntegrateWise was built to end that. It connects all your tools into one workspace, builds a single source of truth from every connected system, and gives you an AI — the Twin — that reads across everything before it speaks.

---

## How It Works

**Connect your tools** — 70+ integrations across CRM, billing, support, analytics, marketing, email, chat, docs, project management, and more. One-time OAuth setup. No data migration. No engineering team.

**Get a single source of truth** — Data from every tool is automatically cleaned, deduplicated, and organized into one unified record per entity. "Acme Corp" in Salesforce, "Acme Corporation" in Zendesk, and "acme.com" in Stripe become one record — with every interaction, payment, ticket, and conversation linked together.

**The Twin reads three layers** — Not just your CRM data. The Twin reads:

- Your data — accounts, contacts, deals, invoices, tasks, tickets from every connected tool
- Your conversations — emails, Slack messages, meeting notes, documents
- Your knowledge — AI session summaries, decisions, and insights that accumulate over time and are governed by your approval

**The Twin surfaces what matters** — It watches patterns across all your tools and tells you what needs attention. "This account's usage dropped 42% and their renewal is in 45 days." "This client's GST filing is overdue and they haven't responded in 3 weeks." "This customer hasn't ordered in 30 days." Every insight comes with evidence — which data, from which tool, how confident.

**You approve before anything happens** — The Twin proposes actions. You see the evidence and the reasoning. You approve, modify, or dismiss. Nothing executes without your say. When an action runs, the result flows back in — the Twin learns from your decisions.

## The Story Behind It

The founder spent 13 years as a MuleSoft Integration Architect wiring enterprise systems across telecom, finance, healthcare, and retail. As a Customer Success Manager at Salesforce, he inherited an $8M account marked 100% attrition risk — with zero handover context. He manually cross-referenced 7 systems for weeks and found two signals no dashboard could see: an unsigned contract buried between systems and an interpersonal conflict hidden in fragmented records. He saved the account. Then he built IntegrateWise so nobody would ever have to do that by hand again.
