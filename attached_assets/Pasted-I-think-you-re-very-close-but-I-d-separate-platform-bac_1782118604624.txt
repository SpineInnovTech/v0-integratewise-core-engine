I think you're very close, but I'd separate **platform backend** from **Customer Zero operations** a little more than you're describing.

Because if Customer Zero owns auth, tenants, schema, and platform governance, then you've accidentally made:

```text id="z34fow"
Customer Zero
=
Platform
```

again.

And you've spent weeks trying to get away from that.

---

I'd structure it like this:

## Repo 1 — integratewise-live (Platform)

This is the canonical backend.

```text id="mwl6z5"
integratewise-live
=================

Identity
├── Clerk
├── Auth
├── RBAC
├── Organizations
└── Sessions

Tenant Platform
├── Tenant Registry
├── Tenant Provisioning
├── Tenant Policies
├── Tenant Capabilities
└── Tenant Lifecycle

Schema Platform
├── Schema AI
├── Schema Pool
├── Entity Models
├── Validation
└── Schema Provider

Spine
├── Entities
├── Memory
├── Signals
├── Knowledge
└── Relationships

Platform Services
├── Connectors
├── Nango
├── MCP
├── Governance
├── Continuity
├── Execution
└── Twin Runtime
```

This repo owns truth.

---

## Repo 2 — Customer Zero

Customer Zero becomes a **platform client**.

```text id="5l2q7x"
iwa-customer-zero
=================

Operations
├── CRM
├── Customer Success
├── Projects
├── Tasks
├── Metrics
├── Knowledge
└── Integrations

Platform Operations
├── Tenant Dashboard
├── Billing Dashboard
├── Usage
├── Connector Health
├── Execution Center
└── AI Operations
```

This is your operating surface.

---

### The critical distinction

Customer Zero should be allowed to manage:

```text id="z7m0w4"
Tenants
Users
Billing
Approvals
```

through APIs.

But it shouldn't own them.

The ownership stays here:

```text id="5hnx0g"
integratewise-live
```

Because later you'll have:

```text id="0hm3fd"

```


against the same platform.

---

### In one sentence

I would make:

```text id="l0f75m"
integratewise-live
```

the **platform that owns identity, tenancy, schema, memory, governance, and execution**.

And make:

```text id="6f2d6h"
Customer Zero
```

the **first and most privileged consumer of that platform**, containing your SaaS admin panel, operational dashboards, CRM, customer success, billing views, and company operating surface.

That gives you:

```text id="v9wl8n"
One backend
Many projections
Customer Zero is a tenant
Customer Zero is also the admin client
```

which fits both your platform doctrine and your desire to preserve the operational surface you've already built.
