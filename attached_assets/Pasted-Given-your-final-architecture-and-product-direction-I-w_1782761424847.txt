# Final Rule

Every frontend follows the same contract:

```
Frontend
    │
    ├── Projection Manifest
    ├── Gateway SDK
    └── Design System
            │
            ▼
       IntegrateWise Gateway
            │
     Projection Facade
            │
     Continuity Bridge
```

This aligns well with your product strategy because customers can buy **individual projection applications** (such as Account Success or Business Intelligence) without purchasing the entire platform, while all of them continue to run on the same shared backend, Adaptive Spine, governance, and Continuity Bridge. This gives you a single backend with multiple sellable products, rather than one monolithic application.
