Frontend Contract

Every frontend receives exactly one projection.

const projection = await sdk.projection.load({
  projection: "workspace",
  tenantId,
  userId,
  role,
  device,
});


The frontend renders the returned projection. It never orchestrates backend services or composes business data.

Final Principles

One Gateway — all clients communicate through a single gateway.

One Spine — canonical business truth lives in the Adaptive Spine.

One Projection Contract — frontends consume projections, not services.

Projection Facades — compose capabilities into frontend-ready views.

Replaceable Frontends — any UI can be swapped without backend changes.

Cloudflare-only Product Plane — all core infrastructure remains on Cloudflare.

Backend Owns Intelligence — AI, memory, governance, and business logic stay server-side.

Frontend Owns Presentation — UI renders data and handles interaction only.

With this, the backend architecture is effectively frozen. Future work should focus on implementing capabilities and projections rather than introducing new foundational layers.
