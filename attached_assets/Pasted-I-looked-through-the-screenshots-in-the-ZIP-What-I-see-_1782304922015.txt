I looked through the screenshots in the ZIP.

What I see is a flow that looks like:

### Current Onboarding Flow

1. **Choose workspace type**

   * Personal
   * Work
   * Organization

2. **Organization details**

   * Company information
   * Team size
   * Industry
   * Department

3. **Primary goals**

   * User selects objectives
   * Business outcomes
   * Operational focus

4. **Connect tools**

   * SaaS applications
   * Data sources
   * Productivity tools

5. **Workspace generated**

   * Dashboard
   * Overview
   * Tasks
   * Operational views

---

### What I Like

The flow is simple.

A new user can understand:

```text
Who am I?
↓
What organization am I in?
↓
What am I trying to achieve?
↓
What tools do I use?
↓
Give me my workspace
```

That's much easier than exposing:

```text
Schema
Entities
Memory
Spine
Vector DB
Twin
Governance
Workflows
```

on day one.

---

### What I Think Is Missing

Based on everything you've described for IntegrateWise.ai, there is one critical missing step:

```text
Goals & Strategy
```

Not because humans need it.

Because AI needs it.

Today the flow looks like:

```text
Identity
↓
Tools
↓
Workspace
```

But IntegrateWise actually works more like:

```text
Identity
↓
Goals & Strategy
↓
Tools
↓
Schema Generation
↓
Workspace
```

Because:

* Goals determine what matters
* Strategy determines what signals matter
* Connectors determine where data comes from
* Schema determines how truth is stored

Without goals, AI only knows:

```text
what happened
```

not

```text
what the company is trying to achieve
```

---

### Another Missing Piece

The connector screen should not be:

```text
Connect Apollo
Connect Slack
Connect Gmail
```

only.

It should also ask:

```text
What should this connector be shared with?
```

Example:

```text
Apollo
✓ Sales Workspace
✓ Founder Workspace
✓ Twin
✓ Account Research
✓ CRM Projection
```

Because in your architecture:

```text
Connectors
≠ Apps

Connectors
=
Capabilities feeding the Spine
```

One connection can power many projections.

---

### The Biggest Difference From Typical SaaS

Most SaaS products do:

```text
Tool
↓
UI
↓
User
```

IntegrateWise should do:

```text
Tool
↓
Spine
↓
Role
↓
Workspace Projection
↓
User
```

Meaning:

A Billing Operator and an Accountant may have:

```text
Same Stripe
Same QuickBooks
Same Company
Same Data
```

but completely different workbenches.

Because humans think in:

```text
Responsibilities
Duties
Goals
```

not entities.

That's the nuance you've been repeating.

---

### What I Would Keep Hidden

Do NOT show these during onboarding:

```text
Spine
Schema AI
Memory Lifecycle
Continuity Engine
Entity Resolution
Twin Runtime
Vector Search
Governance Engine
```

Those belong to the platform.

The user should only see:

```text
Who are you?
↓
What are you responsible for?
↓
What are you trying to achieve?
↓
What tools do you use?
↓
Here's your workspace
```

Everything else happens behind the scenes.

That aligns much more closely with the direction you've been describing: **the workspace is the product; the architecture is the implementation.**
