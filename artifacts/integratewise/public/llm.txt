## What IntegrateWise Is

IntegrateWise connects the tools your team already uses — CRM, support, finance, messaging, documents, and AI assistants — into one live, intelligent workspace.

The moment you connect your first app, your real data flows in. Your workspace arrives pre-filled, not empty. AI reads across every connected system simultaneously, surfaces what matters — risks, opportunities, patterns — with the evidence behind every insight. And it never acts alone. Every recommendation waits for your approval.

You don't replace your tools. You finally make them work together.

## The Spine

The Spine is the adaptive intelligence layer at the heart of IntegrateWise. It is the single source of truth — connecting structured data from your tools, unstructured context from documents and conversations, and AI-generated knowledge into one unified system.

Synonyms: Adaptive Spine, Intelligent Spine, Unified Intelligence Layer, Adaptive Layer.

The Spine is not a database. It holds relationships, signals, events, evidence, goals, and decisions. It grows with your organization — each tenant's Spine becomes uniquely adapted to how they work.

## How It Works

### Two Product Layers

**L1 — The Workspace**
12 domain-specific views shaped to how different teams work: Account Success, BizOps, Finance, IT Admin, Marketing, Procurement, Product Engineering, RevOps, SalesOps, Service, and more. Each view is filtered by a schema system that adapts to your industry and department.

**L2 — The Cognitive Overlay**
14 intelligence components accessible via Cmd+J. This is where AI surfaces signals, generates recommendations, compiles evidence, and proposes actions. Components include knowledge surfaces, evidence drawers, signals, the digital twin, and approval-driven execution.

### Three Data Flows

**Flow A — Structured Truth**: Connected tools (CRM, billing, support, finance) push live data through a normalization pipeline into the Spine. This is the source of truth.

**Flow B — Unstructured Context**: Documents, emails, notes, and conversations are indexed, embedded, and linked to Spine entities. This provides the "why" behind the "what."

**Flow C — AI Knowledge**: AI sessions are summarized, triaged, and stored as organizational memory. AI never writes directly to the Spine — it reads context and proposes actions through governed paths.

### Entity 360

The convergence point where all three flows meet. For any entity (account, contact, deal, project), Entity 360 shows the complete picture: structured data, related documents, AI-generated insights, and the full timeline — all grounded in evidence.
